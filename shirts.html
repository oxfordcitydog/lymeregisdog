Shirts

Wearable art editions — shirts printed with artworks from Blueprints of Technology, made to order in the UK and posted directly to you. Every design is a limited edition of five — once the five are worn, that design is retired. Each is £200 including UK delivery (UK addresses only). Images below are low-resolution previews marked “SAMPLE”; the printed shirt carries the full artwork, no watermark. Prefer the real thing? Browse the original prints.

How to order: press Buy, and when PayPal asks for a note, please include your size (S, M, L, XL or 2XL) and your UK delivery address. Please choose “goods and services” when paying. Each shirt is printed for you on demand, so please allow up to 20 days for it to arrive in the post. The button shirt is a lightweight woven polyester (the fabric that makes all-over printing possible), printed, cut and sewn to order; the tees are soft organic cotton. Made-to-order items can be returned within 14 days of delivery if faulty or not as described.
